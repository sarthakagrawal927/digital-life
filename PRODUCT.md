# Digital Life

Digital Life helps researchers test whether inherited executable strategies sustain viable populations by running bounded, reproducible resource-constrained worlds and matched program replays.

The executable program determines behavior. Replication is supplied, not evolved from scratch. Diversity and visual complexity are observations, not proof of adaptation or invention.

The first experiment compares otherwise identical mutation-on/off worlds. Candidate descendants are screened by terminal abundance and tested against their founding ancestor with mutation disabled on held-out seeds. These exploratory replays do not establish statistical significance or open-ended invention.

This is an owner-local research MVP. The engine and experiment runner are implemented. The owner selected B — Observatory. The live Observatory is implemented locally: seeded worker execution, controls, inspection, resource accounting, replay, configuration, browser comparisons and exports. World 1 stress comparisons are reproducible headlessly. The earlier direction probes remain recorded-data design evidence. No public launch, deployment, LLM inhabitants, communication or persistent structures.
