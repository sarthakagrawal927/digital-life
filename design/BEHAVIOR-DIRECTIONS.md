Digital Life helps researchers test whether inherited executable strategies sustain viable populations by running bounded, reproducible resource-constrained worlds and matched program replays. This sentence matches PRODUCT.md. The operating surface must let a first-time observer understand action, inheritance, and outcomes without reading bytecode. ## A — Microscope

- Purpose/audience/job: let an observer understand one executable life before reading population-level effects.
- Thesis: a warm laboratory bench with a large literal close-up, a six-step founder routine, and a saved-world scrubber.
- Layout/type: two equal reading areas; Georgia display headings, Helvetica explanations, Menlo indexing; stack on phones.
- Color roles: ivory surface, forest text and habitat, green source cells, amber selected organism.
- Interaction: proposed live instruction stepping would synchronize the highlighted routine, local field action and reserve change. The preview shows real 5×5 surroundings and selected reserves; it does not claim an executed action trace.
- Signature: the same organism links instruction, physical action, reserve and offspring.
- Risk: close-ups hide the ecosystem. Implementation would include a clear world overview and stable selection.
- Preview: behavior-a.html.

## B — Living atlas

- Purpose/audience/job: let an observer follow ecological causes while watching the whole population.
- Thesis: full habitat first, explained by visible action traces and contextual cause-and-effect annotations.
- Layout/type: wide habitat plus narrative rail; bold Helvetica heading, Georgia narrative, Menlo coordinates/readings; stacked field and explanation on phones.
- Color roles: deep forest surface, pale organism marks, green sources, amber selection/actions; deaths need a distinct symbol as well as color.
- Interaction: proposed live movement trails, harvest transfers and parent-to-child links derived exclusively from engine events. Preview uses actual population/birth/death counts at the scrubbed state.
- Signature: see energy leave a patch, enter a reserve, and fund a copy.
- Risk: busy worlds overwhelm. Implementation would emphasize a followed individual and aggregate background activity rather than show every effect simultaneously.
- Preview: behavior-b.html.

## C — Evolution theatre

- Purpose/audience/job: let an observer answer “what did the descendant do differently?” and distinguish persistence from reproductive output.
- Thesis: two equally weighted programs under matched conditions, plain-language instruction differences, and explicit outcome evidence.
- Layout/type: paired stages and a shared verdict; Helvetica questions, Georgia numeric outcomes, Menlo programs; consecutive labeled stages on phones.
- Color roles: ink surface, cool ancestor stage, warm descendant stage, matching semantic labels.
- Interaction: proposed synchronized replay plus instruction differences. The preview contains computed same-seed, mutation-disabled results and clearly labeled instruction-loop diagrams, not movement recordings.
- Signature: an inherited loss of reproduction explains zero offspring despite survival.
- Risk: a dramatic verdict may overstate one test. Always label seed, horizon, compute limits, discovery versus held-out evaluation, and uncertainty.
- Preview: behavior-c.html.

Recommendation: A for the first experience because it reveals what an action means before speeding up. C supplies the strongest explanation of inherited behavioral differences; it could become the later experiment view after selection.
